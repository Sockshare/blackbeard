PanelForge: Studio Ghibli & AI Comic Book / Manga Studio

Summary

PanelForge is an end-to-end AI comic book, graphic novel, and shonen manga creation studio. Writers pitch a concept, select an art style (Studio Ghibli, Shonen Manga, American Superhero, Cyberpunk Noir, Watercolor Fable, or Franco-Belgian Ligne Claire), and PanelForge scripts multi-page issues while synthesizing bespoke panel artwork with dynamic speedlines, cel-shaded characters, atmospheric lighting, and movable SVG speech balloons, thought clouds, and SFX lettering.

Key Features

  • Inline AI Comic Book Creator & Multi-Page Scripting: One-click top creation bar lets creators type any title, premise, art style, and page layout (`4-Panel Classic`, `6-Panel Grid`, `Manga Action Diagonal`, `Cinematic Widescreen`, or `Splash Page`) to script and illustrate a complete comic issue.
  • On-the-Fly Panel, Page & Full-Issue AI Artwork Generation: Synthesizes bespoke visual compositions per panel with cryptographic variation seeds, camera-angle framing (`extreme wide`, `low-angle heroic`, `dutch tilt`, `extreme close-up`), weather particles, and optional live Pollinations Flux neural backdrops.
  • Character Sheet Vault & Visual Consistency: Maintains persistent character sheets (costume palette, hair silhouette, signature props, and archetype) that feed directly into every panel's rendering pipeline.
  • Interactive Lettering & Director Inspector: Click any panel to edit scene beats, camera angles, dialogue balloons (`speech`, `thought`, `caption`, `shout`), or explosive sound effects (`BOOM!`, `fwoosh`), and hit 'Generate Panel AI Art' to re-render on the spot.
  • Multi-Format Publishing (Page, Webtoon, HTML & CBZ): Switch seamlessly between traditional comic page spreads and vertical Webtoon scroll mode, or export standalone HTML issues and standard `.cbz` comic archive bundles.

Tech Stack

  • Comic Director & Art Engine: Python, FastAPI, Procedural + Neural SVG/Image Panel Compositor, 6 Art-Style Shaders, Seeded Variation Pipeline
  • Studio Frontend: Flexbox Zero-Clip Studio Workspace, Interactive SVG Panel Canvas, Live Balloon & SFX Inspector, Webtoon Vertical Scroll Mode
  • Archiving & Persistence: SQLite Issue & Character Vault, ZIP/CBZ Comic Book Archive Builder, Print-Ready HTML Exporter
  • Verification: Pytest Pipeline Suite + Playwright Full-Viewport Layout & Live Art Re-Roll Verification

Theory & Architecture

Creating sequential art requires tight coupling between narrative pacing, camera cinematography, character continuity, and lettering placement. PanelForge separates comic creation into a structured Director Engine (which plans page layouts, panel beats, camera angles, and dialogue) and a deterministic + neural Renderer that composites layered environments, characters, action lines, and comic typography into crisp, editable vector-hybrid pages.
