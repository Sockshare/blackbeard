Fix-It Assistant: Safety-First Home, Car & Appliance Repair Guide

Summary

Fix-It Assistant is a local-first diagnostic and repair companion designed for everyday homeowners, renters, and drivers. Users describe what's wrong in plain words—or attach a photo—and the assistant cross-references 45+ hand-written repair guides across 9 categories (Plumbing, Electrical, Appliances, Heating/Cooling, Doors/Windows, Walls/Floors, Outdoor, Car, and Bike). Deterministic safety rules check for life-safety emergencies first and never allow an AI model to recommend dangerous DIY work.

Key Features

  • Unconditional Life-Safety Red-Flag Engine: Screens every input first for gas leaks, carbon monoxide alarms, electrical arcing/fire, water near breaker panels, sewage backups, burst pipes, and structural collapse—immediately displaying emergency shutoff steps.
  • Interactive Cause Narrowing & Plain-English Guides: Asks 1–3 simple follow-up questions to re-rank likely causes, then presents required tools, DIY vs. Pro cost estimates, numbered checkboxes, and clear 'Stop & Call a Pro If' boundaries.
  • Multimodal Photo Inspection (Local & Cloud): Supports local vision models via Ollama (`moondream`, `llava`) as well as OpenAI and Gemini vision to describe corrosion, leaks, or worn parts while keeping uploads in RAM.
  • Safety-Guarded Follow-Up Q&A: Answers user questions grounded strictly in the active repair card, with a post-generation safety filter that blocks hazardous DIY instructions (torsion springs, microwave capacitors, gas valves, refrigerant).
  • 92-Test Verification & Grade-6 Readability: Verified by 92 automated unit, smoke, and Playwright browser tests plus an automated Flesch-Kincaid readability gate (worst doc grade 6.4).

Tech Stack

  • Diagnostic & Safety Core: Python, FastAPI, Deterministic Stem/Anchor Matcher, Hard-Coded Emergency Red-Flag Regex Engine
  • Vision & LLM Providers: Offline Rules Engine, Local Ollama (`moondream` Vision + `llama3.2:3b` Text), OpenAI (`gpt-4o-mini`), Google Gemini (`gemini-2.5-flash`)
  • Privacy & Security: 127.0.0.1 Loopback Binding, RAM-Only Photo Parsing, Cross-Site Write Protection, 0600 Local Notebook Storage
  • Verification: 92 Pytest + Playwright Tests, Automated Readability Audit, Live Ollama Vision E2E Verification

Theory & Architecture

General-purpose chatbots are dangerous for physical repairs because they cheerfully improvise steps around mains voltage, gas lines, and high-tension garage springs. Fix-It Assistant inverts the architecture: a curated, human-vetted repair knowledge base and hard-coded safety state machine own every diagnosis, safety rating, and step—while vision and language models are restricted to describing photos, ranking candidate matches, and answering questions inside strict safety guardrails.
