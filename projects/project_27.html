Eureka Lab: Autonomous Scientific Discovery & Physics Workbench

Summary

Eureka Lab is an autonomous scientific research and symbolic law-discovery workbench. Researchers select an interactive physical system (`Nonlinear Double Pendulum`, `Lotka-Volterra Predator-Prey`, `SIR Epidemic Dynamics`, `Lorenz Chaotic Attractor`, `Driven Damped Harmonic Oscillator`, or custom CSV experimental telemetry), run noisy numerical simulations, execute Pareto-optimal symbolic regression to rediscover the governing differential equations from raw data, and convene a 5-agent scientific review board to publish a complete LaTeX-formatted research manuscript.

Key Features

  • Interactive RK4 Dynamical Systems & Phase-Space Simulator: Integrates coupled nonlinear differential equations in real time with configurable sensor noise, initial conditions, and parameter sweeps—rendering synchronized time-series trajectories and 2D/3D phase-space portraits.
  • Pareto-Optimal Symbolic Regression Engine: Searches candidate mathematical operator trees (`+`, `-`, `*`, `/`, `sin`, `cos`, `exp`, polynomials) via regularized sparse regression (SINDy-style) to uncover interpretable governing equations ranked along the accuracy-vs-complexity Pareto frontier (`R²` vs. MDL complexity).
  • Closed-Loop Active Experiment Designer: Proposes next-best experimental parameter perturbations to disambiguate competing symbolic hypotheses where model uncertainty is highest.
  • 5-Agent Scientific Discovery & Peer-Review Pipeline: Orchestrates `Theorist`, `Experimentalist`, `Symbolic Mathematician`, `Skeptical Referee`, and `Principal Investigator` agents to critique residuals, verify conservation laws, and synthesize formal findings.
  • Publication-Grade Manuscript & LaTeX Exporter: Compiles discovered equations, residual diagnostics, phase-space figures, and referee rebuttals into a downloadable `.tex` / HTML scientific paper.

Tech Stack

  • Scientific Compute & Symbolic Core: Python, FastAPI, Runge-Kutta (RK4) ODE Solver, SINDy-Style Sparse Symbolic Regression, Pareto Frontier Evaluator
  • Multi-Agent Research Board: 5-Role Adversarial Scientific Pipeline (Theorist, Experimentalist, Mathematician, Referee, PI) with Ollama / OpenAI / Gemini Support
  • Interactive Lab Telemetry UI: HTML5 Canvas Phase-Space & Time-Series Oscilloscope, Pareto Complexity Plot, Live LaTeX Equation Inspector
  • Verification: Pytest Symbolic Recovery Suite (`R² > 0.99` on benchmark ODEs) + Playwright Lab Telemetry Verification

Theory & Architecture

Deep neural networks can fit trajectory data, but they act as opaque black boxes that violate physical conservation laws outside their training distribution. Eureka Lab combines sparse symbolic regression with adversarial multi-agent peer review to distill raw, noisy sensor trajectories into compact, human-interpretable differential equations—automating the full scientific method from hypothesis and experiment to mathematical law and peer-reviewed manuscript.
