ElectroForge 3D: Interactive Electronics, Breadboard & Robotics Lab

Summary

ElectroForge 3D is an interactive 3D electronics, solderless breadboard, microcontroller, and robotics engineering workbench (Port 8102). Powered by a real-time Modified Nodal Analysis (MNA) & thermal physics engine (circuit_engine.py) paired with 12 pre-baked Blender 5.0.1 .glb PBR hardware models, it lets engineers wire circuits to 4 articulated 3D robotic rigs, probe signals on a 4-channel oscilloscope and 8-channel logic analyzer, inject 24V overload burnout faults, and export SPICE netlists (.cir), Bills of Materials (.csv), and Arduino C++ firmware (.ino).

Key Features

  • 12 Blender 5.0.1 PBR .glb Hardware Models & 4 Articulated 3D Rigs: Wire an 830-Tie-Point Breadboard and Arduino Uno R3 / ESP32 alongside axial resistors, electrolytic capacitors, 555 DIP-8 ICs, TO-220 MOSFETs, 5mm LEDs, and HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensors to a 6-DOF Industrial Robot Arm, 4WD Ultrasonic Rover, Pan/Tilt Solar & Laser Turret, or 3-Axis Stepper CNC Gantry.
  • Real-Time MNA Solver, Joule Heating & 24V Overload Burnout: Computes nodal voltages, RC transients, 555 astable timing, Joule heating (P = I²R, T = T_amb + P · R_θ), and destructive component failure, complete with 5 live 3D visual overlays (electron particle flow, FLIR thermal heatmap, overload burnout VFX, magnetic field lines, and voltage-gradient wires).
  • Virtual Test Bench & Environmental Stimuli: Features a dual-rail Bench PSU, Arbitrary Waveform Generator (SINE, PWM_SQUARE, TRIANGLE, SAWTOOTH), 4-Ch Oscilloscope, 8-Ch Logic Analyzer, Digital Multimeter probes, and interactive 3D stimuli (flashlight lux, ultrasonic obstacle distance, heatgun temperature, rotary potentiometer, and tactile pushbutton).
  • 6 Starter Labs, Fault Injection & 1:1 Command Bus + CLI: Switch between 6 starter labs (lab_robot_arm, lab_555_chaser, lab_rover_ultrasonic, lab_solar_turret, lab_overload_burnout, lab_blank_sandbox), inject faults (SHORT_VCC_GND), or automate every action via POST /api/dispatch and cli.py.
  • Engineering Exports: One-click downloads for SPICE netlists (/api/export/spice), CSV Bills of Materials (/api/export/bom), and starter Arduino C++ firmware sketches (/api/export/firmware).

Tech Stack

  • Physics & Circuit Core: Python 3 + Flask (server.py), Modified Nodal Analysis (MNA) solver, Joule thermal & overload state machine, 4-Ch Scope & 8-Ch Logic waveform synthesis (circuit_engine.py)
  • 3D CAD & WebGL Rendering: Headless Blender 5.0.1 bmesh + Principled BSDF .glb asset pipeline (blender_electronics_pipeline.py), Three.js + EffectComposer + UnrealBloomPass
  • Automation & Exports: Unified POST /api/dispatch 1:1 command bus, cli.py terminal controller (status, load-lab, psu, fault, repair, export-spice, export-bom)
  • Visual Modes: 4 studio themes (precision_lab, cyber_neon, flir_thermal, xray_emf) and 4 time-dilation scales (1.0x, 0.1x, 0.01x, paused)

Engineering Architecture

Instead of decoupling 3D visual animation from circuit math, ElectroForge 3D routes every UI slider, environmental stimulus, and CLI command through a single thread-safe ElectroForgeLabEngine.dispatch() state machine. Each mutation re-solves nodal voltages, branch currents, thermal dissipation, oscilloscope buffers, and robotic joint kinematics in lockstep—so cranking the PSU to 24.0V or moving a 3D ultrasonic obstacle physically alters both electrical waveforms and 3D actuator motion.
