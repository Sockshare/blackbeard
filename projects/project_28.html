BattleBot Designer: 3D Combat-Robot CAD Studio, Factory Floor & Arena Simulator

Summary

BattleBot Designer is an interactive 3D combat-robot engineering workbench, piece-by-piece Factory Floor assembler, and Arena combat simulator. Every robot is modeled as a connected graph: 17 chassis architectures expose five universal hardpoints (Front, Top, Rear, Left, Right), any of 36 kinetic, control, thermal, electrical, projectile, or passive modules can bolt onto any mount, and a swept-volume interference solver automatically blocks only combinations whose motion envelopes or lines of fire physically clash.

Key Features

  • 17 Chassis × 36 Hardpoint Modules Across 7 Weight Classes: Span from 1 lb Antweight to 250 lb Heavyweight (including 2-bot cluster pairs), with quadruped and hexapod walkers, twin rubber tracks, U-frame and H-frame weapon bays, delta hulls, and melty-brain pucks.
  • First-Principles Electrical, Drive & Spin-Up Physics: Simulates spinner moment of inertia, aerodynamic drag, motor back-EMF spin-up curves, battery internal-resistance voltage sag, isolated vs. shared buses, wire AWG sizing, and full-body shell slip-ring / hollow-mast mechanics.
  • Swept-Volume Interference Solver & Per-Face Armor: Computes 3D bounding envelopes across full flipper, hammer, crusher, and turret arcs to prevent physical self-collisions, while letting builders tune plate stock (AR500, Hardox, Grade 5 Titanium, 7075-T6, CFRP, UHMW) per face.
  • 12-Station Factory Floor & Piece-Level Teardown: Lower each subsystem in via overhead hoist station by station, or scrub the teardown slider to separate assemblies down to individual motor bells, stators, ESC heat sinks, and fasteners with zero spin wobble.
  • Interactive Arena Combat Simulator: Drive your custom build with WASD and fire multi-mount weapons with SPACE + directional arrows against 3-tier AI opponents across kill saws, wall screws, flame jets, and a timed pit, complete with 3-judge scorecards and instant replay.

Tech Stack

  • Engineering & Combat Core: Vanilla JavaScript (UMD Node/Browser), First-Principles Drivetrain & Spinner ODE Integrator, Swept-Volume AABB Clash Solver, 30 Hz Seeded Arena Simulation
  • Dual 3D Rendering Pipeline: Three.js r128 WebGL Pipeline + Custom Span-Rasterized CPU Z-Buffer Renderer with Gouraud PBR Shading & Projected Ground Shadows
  • Backend & Export Suite: Python, Flask, Shareable Base64URL Build Links, CSV Bill of Materials with Cost Estimator, Printable Spec Sheet & Tale of the Tape PNG Exporter
  • Verification: Automated Node Test Suite (tools/test.sh) verifying 3,060 physics combos, 5,741 3D builds, 0.00 mm exploded-spin wobble, and 25 seeded AI-vs-AI arena matches

Theory & Architecture

Most robot configurators lock specific weapons to specific hulls with arbitrary rules. BattleBot Designer instead treats the robot as a physical graph governed by conservation of mass, current limits, center-of-gravity support polygons, and 3D swept-volume geometry—allowing any wild Frankenstein combination that physically fits while immediately surfacing the real engineering trade-offs in weight, voltage sag, gyroscopic precession, and spin-up time.
