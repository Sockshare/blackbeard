Operation Apollo

Overview

Operation Apollo is an AI-driven market intelligence and stock advisory system designed to synthesize quantitative price action and qualitative news sentiment into data-driven insights. Leveraging machine learning models, LLM reasoning, and live market APIs, Apollo tailors analysis to individual risk tolerance across both short-term momentum setups and long-term portfolio horizons.

Key Capabilities & Agentic Features

  • AI-Powered Market & Sentiment Analysis: Combines historical time-series indicators with LLM-parsed market news to surface high-signal catalysts.
  • Risk-Adjusted Strategy Personalization: Tailors position sizing and sector exposure insights to user-defined volatility and drawdown thresholds.
  • Real-Time Alerting & Anomaly Detection: Monitors live tickers for volume breakouts, moving-average crossovers, and macro events.
  • Dual-Horizon Intelligence: Supports both intraday/swing technical setups and long-term fundamental compounding analysis.
  • Interactive Financial Visualizations: Renders interactive candlestick charts, correlation heatmaps, and portfolio exposure breakdowns.
  • Encrypted Cloud Architecture: Secured with OAuth 2.0, HTTPS/TLS, and encrypted PostgreSQL persistence.

Architecture & Tech Stack

  • Frontend: React.js, Interactive Charting Libraries
  • Backend: Python (ML/Analytics), Flask / REST APIs
  • AI & Data: OpenAI / LLM APIs, Alpha Vantage & Yahoo Finance Market Feeds
  • Infrastructure: PostgreSQL, Cloud Compute, OAuth 2.0 & AES Encryption

Engineering Philosophy & Systems Theory

Operation Apollo democratizes quantitative market workflows by pairing statistical time-series screening with LLM synthesis. Instead of drowning retail analysts in raw noisy tickers, the platform distills multi-factor signals into transparent, risk-aware decision briefs.
