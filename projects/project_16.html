Oracle Desk: Prediction-Market Intelligence Terminal

Summary

Oracle Desk is a quantitative prediction-market research and paper-trading terminal engineered for transparent edge discovery. It ingests live markets from Polymarket and Kalshi without requiring paid data feeds or trading accounts, pairs them against independent statistical reference models (FRED economic indicators, NOAA weather forecasts, CoinGecko implied volatility, and GDELT news velocity), and runs a 5-agent adversarial pipeline where a mandatory Skeptic agent attacks the Quant agent's fair-value thesis before any paper trade is logged.

Key Features

  • Cross-Venue Arbitrage & Spread Scanner: Matches equivalent contracts across Polymarket and Kalshi using token-similarity plus strict polarity, numeric-strike, and settlement-date guards—reporting net spreads after Kalshi quadratic taker fees and orderbook depth penalties.
  • Transparent Reality Reference Models: Computes independent fair-value probabilities and 90% confidence intervals from live FRED macro time series, NOAA Weather.gov hourly gridpoints, CoinGecko log-normal spot drift, and GDELT news tone.
  • 5-Agent Adversarial Research Pipeline: Orchestrates Scout, Evidence Researcher, Bayesian Quant, mandatory Skeptic (steel-manning the market price), and Trade Journal agents with full step-by-step provenance.
  • Whale Tape & Anomaly Detection: Flags statistically outsized trades (robust MAD z-score > 2.5 and notional thresholds) across live venue trade tapes.
  • Brier-Scored Paper Portfolio & Calibration Lab: Tracks virtual YES/NO positions, unrealized PnL, and proper Brier Score decomposition (reliability, resolution, uncertainty) against market baselines.

Tech Stack

  • Backend & Quant Engine: Python, FastAPI, SQLite WAL Telemetry Cache, NumPy / SciPy Calibration & Log-Normal Pricing
  • Live Data Connectors: Polymarket Gamma & CLOB APIs, Kalshi v2 Public API, FRED Macro Series, NOAA Weather.gov, CoinGecko, GDELT
  • Multi-Agent Layer: Deterministic Rule Pipeline + Optional Local Ollama / OpenAI / Gemini Provider Adapter
  • Frontend & Verification: Zero-Build Responsive Terminal UI, SVG Calibration Curves, 40 Automated Pytest + Playwright Gates

Theory & Architecture

Prediction markets aggregate crowd beliefs efficiently on average, yet exhibit systematic microstructure distortions around fee tiers, long-shot bias, stale low-liquidity contracts, and breaking macro releases. By combining fee-aware cross-venue matching with independent non-market reference models and a mandatory Skeptic agent that steel-mans the counterparty view, Oracle Desk turns raw contract prices into an auditable decision-support discipline.
