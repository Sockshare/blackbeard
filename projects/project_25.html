Society in a Bottle: Multi-Agent AI Village & Social Simulation

Summary

Society in a Bottle is a generative multi-agent social simulation and interactive 2D town sandbox. Eight distinct AI citizens—each with Big-Five personality traits, private goals, gold balances, and episodic/reflected memory streams—live, work, converse, spread rumors, and vote at the Town Hall across 9 interactive buildings (`Oakhearth Tavern`, `Town Hall`, `Blacksmith Forge`, `Grand Library`, `Apothecary`, `Market Square`, `Watchtower`, `Moonlit Docks`, and `Chapel Gardens`) while the user intervenes via a live God-Mode miracle/crisis console.

Key Features

  • Live 60fps Interactive 2D Village Sandbox: Renders Oakhearth Village with a winding river, stone bridges, cobblestone paths, swaying pine trees, 9 architectural buildings with warm window glow, animated citizen sprites, and live overhead speech bubbles.
  • Episodic Memory Stream, Reflection & Rumor Propagation: Every observation, conversation, and world event is scored for importance and stored in each citizen's memory stream, triggering high-level reflections and organic word-of-mouth rumor spreading across the social graph.
  • God-Mode Intervention & Miracle Console: Inject global world events (`Festival of Lanterns`, `Gold Rush Discovery`, `Mysterious Plague`, `Tax Decree`) or plant secret whispers directly into a specific citizen's mind and watch the societal ripple effects.
  • Dynamic Social Affinity Matrix & Town Factions: Visualizes evolving trust, rivalry, and alliance edges between all 8 citizens alongside Town Hall law proposals and economic treasury balances.
  • Direct Citizen Interview & Thought Inspector: Click any citizen on the map to inspect their personality sliders, current plan, recent memories, relationship ledger, and interview them directly in first-person.

Tech Stack

  • Multi-Agent Cognitive Architecture: Python, FastAPI, Autonomous Perceive-Plan-Act-Reflect Loop, Importance-Weighted Memory Stream, Social Affinity Graph
  • 2D World & UI Renderer: HTML5 Canvas Village Engine (Day/Dusk/Night Lighting, River & Foliage Shaders, Animated Citizen Avatars, Speech Bubbles), Force/Circular Social Graph
  • LLM & Persistence Layer: Hybrid Deterministic Social Engine + Ollama / OpenAI / Gemini Dialogue Synthesis, SQLite World Timeline Store
  • Verification: Pytest Simulation Suite + Playwright Live Tick, God-Mode Event, and Canvas Rendering Verification

Theory & Architecture

Inspired by Stanford's Generative Agents research, Society in a Bottle demonstrates how complex macro-societal phenomena—factions, information cascades, economic shifts, and emergent norms—arise naturally when individual autonomous agents are equipped with persistent episodic memories, spatial awareness, and pairwise trust ledgers.
