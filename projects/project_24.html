Dungeon Master OS: Autonomous AI Game Master & 3D VTT Battlemap

Summary

Dungeon Master OS is a full-stack AI-driven Dungeons & Dragons 5e Virtual Tabletop (VTT) and autonomous Game Master. It pairs a narrative AI Dungeon Master and rule-enforcing 5e combat engine with a rich interactive HTML5 Canvas tactical battlemap featuring bevelled flagstone dungeon rooms, flickering radial torch sconces, chasm lava hazards, animated water currents, fog of war, A* pathfinding previews, and metallic/wooden token miniatures with live HP arcs.

Key Features

  • Interactive 2.5D Tactical Dungeon Battlemap: Renders multi-room stone dungeons with procedural flagstone textures, 3D wall extrusion shadows, glowing runic altars, flickering wall torches, chasm bridges, and clickable grid coordinates.
  • Autonomous 5e Combat & Monster Tactics Engine: Tracks initiative order, armor class (AC), action economy, spell slots, and status conditions while autonomously executing enemy turns with spatial targeting and attack rolls.
  • Cryptographic Polyhedral Dice Tray (d4–d20): Built-in dice roller supports advantage/disadvantage, ability modifiers, critical hit/fumble detection, and floating damage/heal combat telemetry directly over tokens.
  • AI Narrative Chronicle & Quick-Action Bar: Players can type any freeform roleplay action, click a tile to move, or trigger quick tactical actions (`Attack Nearest`, `Perception Check`, `Cast Sacred Flame`, `Advance Turn`) with rich DM narration.
  • Full Campaign Persistence & Encounter Builder: Supports multiple dungeon maps (`The Sunken Reliquary`, `Emberforge Vault`, `Whispering Catacombs`), custom token spawning, fog-of-war toggles, and SQLite campaign save states.

Tech Stack

  • Rules & AI DM Backend: Python, FastAPI, Deterministic 5e Combat & Dice Resolver, Autonomous Monster Turn AI, Ollama / OpenAI / Gemini DM Narrator
  • Tactical VTT Renderer: HTML5 60fps Canvas Engine, Procedural Stone/Water/Torch Shaders, Metallic & Wooden Token Miniatures, Floating Combat Telemetry
  • State & Realtime Sync: SQLite Campaign Chronicle Store, WebSocket Live Board Broadcast, Instant Zero-Config Demo Campaign Seeding
  • Verification: Pytest Combat/Dice Suite + Headless Playwright Canvas Pixel & Interactive Token Movement Verification

Theory & Architecture

Pure LLM text adventures lose spatial coherence during combat, while traditional Virtual Tabletops require hours of manual map prep and a human Game Master. Dungeon Master OS bridges both worlds: a deterministic 5e spatial engine enforces grid geometry, line-of-sight, initiative, and dice math on a visually atmospheric battlemap, while the AI Game Master translates every roll and tactical maneuver into vivid dark-fantasy storytelling.
