Pelagos OC-8 & 18-DOF Hexapod: 3D Biomimetic Robotics Teardown Field Simulator

Summary

Pelagos OC-8 & 18-DOF Hexapod is an interactive 3D biomimetic robotics field simulator and teardown workbench built on a 1600×900 8-panel engineering HUD. Operators can switch across a 9-unit biomimetic fleet, scrub a continuous 3D teardown slider, isolate individual mechanical sub-assemblies, inspect X-Ray and thermal heatmaps, inject subsystem faults, and export the entire simulator as a self-contained offline HTML bundle.

Key Features

  • 9 Articulated Biomimetic Robotic Units: Includes OC-8 (Robotic Octopus with 202 arm links and 409 suction rollers), HX-18 (18-DOF Six-Legged Walker), SC-8 (Robotic Scorpion), HB-7 (Piezo-Driven Honeybee), CN-12 (12-DOF Quadruped Dog), AV-6 (Peregrine Raptor), AR-8 (8-Legged Tarantula), OP-24 (24-Joint Sidewinder Snake), and AC-14 (Flex-Spine Cheetah).
  • Continuous 3D Teardown & Sub-Assembly Isolation: Explode any unit from 0% to 100% along radial assembly vectors and isolate specific subsystems (OPTICS, MANTLE, ARM R2, SIPHON, BEAK, CHASSIS, SERVOS, CORE) with synchronized 2D screen-space callout leaders.
  • X-Ray, Thermal Heatmap & Fault Injection Lab: Toggle translucent X-Ray housing, inspect actuator thermal load gradients, trigger simulated actuator/seal faults, and cycle through 6 anodized livery arrangements.
  • WebGL Post-Processing + Automatic CPU Canvas Fallback: Renders with Three.js EffectComposer and UnrealBloomPass when GPU acceleration is present, and seamlessly switches to a custom 2D canvas software rasterizer in headless or remote-desktop environments.
  • Zero-Dependency Single-File Offline Exporter: GET /api/export/offline.html inlines WOFF2 fonts, stylesheets, Three.js, and procedural model builders into one portable HTML file.

Tech Stack

  • 3D Procedural Kinematics & Fleet Engine: Vanilla JavaScript (~6,000 LOC across pelagos_sim.js and pelagos_fleet.js), Multi-Joint Forward/Inverse Kinematic Pose Evaluators
  • Rendering Pipeline: Three.js r128, UnrealBloomPass Post-Processing, Custom 2D Canvas Software Projector & Depth-Sorted Rasterizer
  • Telemetry HUD & Audio: 8-Panel 1600×900 Responsive Studio Layout, Web Audio API Synthesized Telemetry & Servo Acoustics
  • Backend & Packaging: Python, Flask, Base64 Asset Inliner for Standalone Offline HTML Export

Theory & Architecture

Inspecting complex multi-DOF robots—like an eight-arm continuum manipulator or an 18-servo hexapod—requires seeing how outer fairings, internal pump cores, and joint actuators relate in space and time. By generating every link, gear, lens, and seal procedurally in code and pairing exploded-view vectors with live kinematic gaits, the simulator lets engineers move fluidly between whole-body motion and component-level inspection.
