Paperwork Pilot: Local-First Document, Bill & Contract Translator

Summary

Paperwork Pilot is a privacy-first document intelligence workstation built for real people facing intimidating paperwork—residential leases, itemized hospital bills, insurance denials, collection notices, and employment agreements. It extracts text locally on-device, flags hidden auto-renewals, junk fees, liability waivers, and hard deadlines, translates every dense clause into clear 6th-grade English with exact page/line citations, and drafts customized dispute or follow-up letters.

Key Features

  • Clause-by-Clause Plain-English Translator: Splits complex contracts and notices into side-by-side cards showing the exact original wording next to a clear, jargon-free explanation.
  • Automated Red-Flag & Trap Scanner: Detects automatic renewal traps, unilateral fee hikes, arbitration waivers, late-fee escalators, and medical billing upcoding/duplicates.
  • Action Checklist & Deadline Extractor: Pulls every date, dollar amount, and required response window into a prioritized checklist so nothing slips through the cracks.
  • One-Click Dispute & Negotiation Letter Generator: Drafts polite, firm, citation-backed letters for security deposit returns, medical bill itemization requests, and contract amendments.
  • Strict Local-First Privacy Architecture: Processes files entirely on localhost (`127.0.0.1`) with local SQLite storage, optional zero-retention mode, and seamless local Ollama support.

Tech Stack

  • Backend & Document Engine: Python, FastAPI, pypdf, Local Rule & Clause Pattern Matcher
  • AI & Readability Pipeline: Local Ollama (`llama3.2`) / Optional OpenAI & Gemini Adapters, Automated Flesch-Kincaid Grade Gate
  • Frontend & Accessibility: Zero-Build Accessible Web UI, Side-by-Side Clause Diff Viewer, Printable Summary Packs
  • Quality Assurance: End-to-End Playwright Browser Suite, Self-Check CLI, Zero-Network Offline Mode

Theory & Architecture

Information asymmetry in consumer contracts, medical billing, and bureaucratic forms costs households thousands of dollars a year simply because legal and billing jargon is designed to discourage scrutiny. By pairing deterministic clause-pattern scanners with grounded local LLM simplification and a strict reading-grade ceiling, Paperwork Pilot gives any individual the analytical leverage of a contract specialist without uploading sensitive personal PDFs to third-party servers.
