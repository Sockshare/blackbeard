WonderBook Studio: AI Children's Storybook Creator & 3D Illustrated Reader

Summary

WonderBook Studio is an interactive AI children's picture book creation studio and 3D illustrated reader. Every storybook, character, and spread is generated on the fly with bespoke AI artwork—combining live Pollinations/Flux neural illustration synthesis, OpenAI/Gemini multimodal pipelines, and a procedural multi-layer Studio Ghibli & glowing watercolor SVG scene compositor so that no two books or spreads ever look the same. Includes an interactive character & art-style wizard, one-click AI Artwork regeneration per page or full book, word-highlighted Web Speech read-aloud narration, and print-ready HTML/PDF storybook export.

Key Features

  • 100% On-the-Fly Bespoke AI Illustration Engine: Generates unique, prompt-seeded artwork for every single book cover and spread—combining live neural image synthesis (Pollinations Flux, OpenAI DALL-E/GPT-Image, or Gemini) with a deep procedural multi-layer watercolor & Studio Ghibli scene renderer so no two books ever share stock art.
  • Interactive 3D Open-Book Spread Reader: Renders tactile hardcover bindings, gold-foil cover typography, drop-cap story pages, and smooth 3D page-turn transitions with keyboard and touch navigation.
  • Per-Spread & Full-Book AI Art Regeneration: Includes live 'Regenerate Spread AI Art' and 'Regenerate All Book Art' controls that increment cryptographic variation seeds to paint fresh skies, biomes, props, and character poses on demand.
  • Synchronized Web Speech Read-Aloud & Word Highlighting: Built-in storyteller narration highlights each word in real time as it is spoken, paired with age-band reading levels (Ages 3–5, 6–8, 9–12).
  • Zero-Config Local Persistence & Printable Keepsakes: Persists every custom-authored book to a local SQLite library and exports self-contained printable HTML/PDF keepsake editions.

Tech Stack

  • Generative Story & Art Core: Python, FastAPI, Dynamic Prompt-Seeded SVG/Canvas Multi-Layer Illustrator, Pollinations Flux / OpenAI / Gemini / Ollama Providers
  • Interactive 3D Book UI: Vanilla ES6 JavaScript, CSS3 3D Perspective Page-Turn Engine, Web Speech API Synthesis, Responsive Hardcover Layout
  • Storage & Export: Local-First SQLite Library, Instant Self-Contained Printable HTML/PDF Book Exporter
  • Verification: Automated Pytest Suite + Headless Playwright Visual & Seed Uniqueness Verification

Theory & Architecture

Most AI story generators pair generic text with static templates or broken image placeholders. WonderBook Studio treats children's publishing as a cohesive visual-narrative pipeline: every spread extracts structured scene descriptors (biome, lighting, weather, hero silhouette, companion pose, and foreground props) and synthesizes a unique, high-resolution illustrated plate on the fly—ensuring every child's story feels like a one-of-a-kind heirloom picture book.
