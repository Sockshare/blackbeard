AeroForge 3D: Fighter Jet CAD, 67-Part Teardown, Mach CFD & 3D Dogfight Simulator

Summary

AeroForge 3D is an interactive 3D aerospace engineering workbench, 67-part mechanical teardown studio, supersonic wind-tunnel solver, and playable 3D dogfight simulator spanning 113 years of combat aviation (1917–2030). Operators can inspect 26 historical and modern fighter aircraft across five eras and three superpowers (USA, RUS, CHN), re-sculpt wing sweep, fuselage chine stealth shaping, engine cycle, and weapons bays in real time, scrub a continuous 0–100% exploded view down to individual turbine stages and AESA radar T/R modules, and fly their custom airframe in live 3D aerial combat.

Key Features

  • 26 Historical & Next-Gen Fighter Presets Across 5 Eras: Span from WWI biplanes (Sopwith Camel, Fokker Dr.I) and WWII piston legends (P-51D Mustang, Yak-3) to early swept-wing jets (F-86F, MiG-15bis), 4th-gen dogfighters (F-15C, F-14D variable-sweep, Su-27, J-10C), 5th-gen stealth (F-22A Raptor, F-35A, Su-57, J-20, J-35A), and 6th-gen tailless/hypersonic concepts (NGAD FX-6, MiG-41, J-36, Darkstar Mach 6 scramjet).
  • 12-Bay / 67-Sub-Part 3D Internal Teardown: Scrub the teardown slider or isolate any of 12 internal engineering bays to inspect nose AESA radar arrays, Martin-Baker ejection seats, titanium wing carry-through bulkheads, bladder fuel tanks, rotating 3-stage fan/compressor/turbine spools, 2D/3D thrust-vectoring petals, and rotary missile launchers.
  • First-Principles ISA Atmosphere, Mach CFD & 360° RCS Solver: Computes lift-curve slope, wave drag across transonic/supersonic Mach regimes, specific excess power ($P_s$), Breguet combat radius, instantaneous/sustained turn rates, and a 360° Radar Cross Section (dBsm) polar diagram sensitive to external pylons vs. internal weapons bays.
  • 5 Visual Inspection Modes & 12-Step Assembly Sequence: Switch seamlessly between Tactical Exterior, Translucent X-Ray + Internal Bays, Mach CFD Pressure Streamlines & Shock Cones, CAD Blueprint Wireframe, and Infrared Thermal Plume Heatmap.
  • Playable 3D Dogfight Arena & Headless CLI Verification: Launch directly from the hangar into a 3D terrain combat arena with W/S throttle & afterburner, pitch/roll flight physics, 20mm rotary cannon, AIM-9X IR and AIM-120D BVR missiles, chaff/flare countermeasures, and an AI bandit—backed by a 1:1 headless CLI and automated test suite.

Tech Stack

  • Aerodynamics & Stealth Core: Vanilla JavaScript (UMD Node/Browser), 1976 ISA Atmosphere Solver, Prandtl-Glauert & Wave-Drag Aerodynamics, 360° RCS Polar Integrator
  • 3D CAD & Dogfight Engine: Three.js r128 PBR Procedural Airframe & 67-Part Subsystem Builder + 2D Canvas Isometric Fallback Renderer, 60 Hz 3D Dogfight Physics & Missile Guidance Loop
  • Backend & Export Suite: Python, Flask, REST Telemetry & Comparison APIs, JSON Spec Exporter, Shareable Base64URL Airframe Links
  • Verification: Automated Node & Headless Chromium Suite (tools/test_aeroforge.js) verifying all 26 presets, 67 sub-parts, 5 view modes, and dogfight combat physics

Theory & Architecture

Rather than loading static pre-baked 3D meshes, AeroForge 3D synthesizes every airframe and all 67 internal sub-parts procedurally from parametric aerodynamic and structural dimensions. Changing wing sweep, aspect ratio, twin- vs. single-engine layout, or internal vs. external ordnance simultaneously updates the 3D geometry, shifts center of gravity and empty weight, recalculates supersonic wave drag and turn performance, and penalizes or restores stealth RCS signature in the live polar plot and 3D dogfight arena.
