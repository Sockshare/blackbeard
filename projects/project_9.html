Autonomous UAR, NoPE & ML Peer-Divergence Engine

Summary

The Autonomous UAR, NoPE & ML Peer-Divergence Engine (paired with Groups Scanner and the Auditor SQL Suite in Run Nexus) reimagines enterprise User Access Reviews and Zero-Standing-Privilege governance. Instead of handing human reviewers flat spreadsheets of hundreds of thousands of opaque group memberships, the engine resolves deeply nested identity graphs using cycle-safe Breadth-First Search (BFS), audits standing human production privileges against strict No-Persistent-Entitlement (NoPE L1–L6), Approval-on-Demand (AoD), and Multi-Party Authorization (MPA) standards, and scores outlier entitlements using a hybrid Machine Learning + Information-Theory divergence model.

Key Features

  • Cycle-Safe BFS Nested-Group Graph Resolution: Traverses multi-hop recursive group hierarchies with cycle detection to expose true transitive effective permissions and privilege inheritance chains.
  • Hybrid Neural + Information-Content Divergence: Combines an unsupervised Neural Autoencoder reconstruction anomaly score with a 7-attribute Information-Content (IC) peer similarity engine across org, role, manager, location, cost center, tenure, and job family.
  • SmartExplain Natural-Language Risk Narratives: Translates mathematical divergence vectors and rare entitlement anomalies into crisp, human-readable audit justifications for security reviewers.
  • Zero-Standing-Privilege (NoPE) Policy Verifier: Automatically flags standing human administrative grants, validating Just-In-Time (JIT) elevation, Multi-Party Authorization (MPA), and break-glass controls.
  • 500K+ Membership Scale & Auditor SQL Suite: Vectorized data pipelines and parameterized SQL audit queries capable of profiling half a million access grants in seconds.

Tech Stack

  • Graph & ML Engine: Python, NumPy, Scikit-Learn / PyTorch Autoencoder, Shannon Information-Content Weighting
  • Identity & SQL Tooling: Cycle-Safe BFS Graph Crawler, Groups Scanner Service, Parameterized Auditor SQL Suite
  • AI Explainability: SmartExplain Feature-Attribution & LLM Risk Summary Synthesis
  • Web Workbench: Flask Interactive Review Console, Outlier Heatmaps, Exportable Compliance Packs

Theory & Architecture

Traditional access reviews suffer from rubber-stamping because humans cannot mentally compute peer-group baselines across thousands of nested entitlements. By uniting deterministic graph theory (to uncover hidden transitive access) with information-theoretic peer clustering and neural anomaly detection, the engine surfaces the exact 1% of entitlements that violate least-privilege—giving auditors mathematical proof and plain-English explanations.
